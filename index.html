01

Closed-Loop DC Motor Speed Control

Integrated capstone · Telkom University · Apr–May 2026 · Team of 6

A full H-bridge built from discrete parts — four MOSFETs (IRF9540 P-channel, IRF540N N-channel) with BD139 gate drivers, freewheeling diodes and a snubber capacitor. ESP32 firmware running 20 kHz PWM and 4× quadrature encoder decoding through dual-channel interrupt service routines, giving 880 effective pulses per revolution.

  • 58%noise cut, filter moved into firmware
  • 12.78RPM RMSE across two ID methods
  • 0.11%steady-state error on hardware
  • ESP32
  • C/C++
  • Power electronics
  • MATLAB
  • Simulink

02

AUDROS — Hydroponic Nutrient & pH Control

Competition project · 2023 · Gold Medal, I2ASPO (IoT category)

An ESP32 system that reads TDS, pH and DS18B20 temperature, then drives DC dosing pumps through relays to hold a hydroponic reservoir in range. Local alerts over a 16×2 I2C LCD and buzzer; remote monitoring through Blynk. Dosing was calibrated against lettuce requirements — pH 6.0–7.0 and 560–840 ppm — after benchmarking mineral, well and municipal water.

  • 80%of assembly and wiring done personally
  • IDR 1.48Mbill of materials, co-authored
  • 2 → 1rejections before the gold medal

Rejected at proposal stage by BRIN and by an international competition in Malaysia. I entered the same system twice more: 2nd place at PNB IT 2023, then Gold at I2ASPO 2023.

  • ESP32
  • Sensor calibration
  • Relay actuation
  • Blynk

An honest position rather than a claim. This section changes as the work does.

01

Two months of using it as a thinking partner

For Studiowebsite.id I've used AI mainly to think out loud — shaping demo site concepts, scripting and editing content, working through ad targeting and how to follow up with leads that go quiet. Useful, but shallow. It was a sounding board, not part of the build.

02

First output is a draft, never the answer

Across the demo sites and the content work, nothing shipped on the first response. The value sits in the second, third and fourth pass. Treating the first output as finished is where most people get burned.
